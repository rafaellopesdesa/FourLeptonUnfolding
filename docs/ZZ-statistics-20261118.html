Source MC and pseudo-experiments

Retain the one-million production target

The existing 411,925 selected events provide 400,146 effective entries, or 384.6 Run 3 equivalents. Even fully completed waves alone provide 397,253 selected / 385,844 effective entries: 370.9 equivalents.

At unchanged weight composition, 1M selected events gives about 971k effective entries / 934 Run 3 equivalents. Its total-yield MC error is about 3.3% of the data statistical error.

The user confirmed retaining 1M per ZZ configuration, then continuing the research program. Global statistics and main mass windows have ample margin for 100 toys; training sufficiency and rare seven-dimensional regions still require closure tests.

100 physical toys contain about 104k ZZ entries in total. Independent resampling draws can share a source template; they also share its finite-MC uncertainty. No production target or physics setting was changed by this assessment.

Matching the data variance

Poisson data and signed bootstraps differ

Physical data require NZZ ∼ Poisson(1040.49), followed by sampling the normalized positive intensity. The count fluctuates with σ = 32.26; fixing exactly 1,040 entries in every toy would remove the total-count fluctuation.

The current signed bootstrap draws approximately 1,048.09 positive and 7.60 negative entries. Its net mean is correct, but its variance is 1,055.69: 1.46% above physical Poisson variance. The Higgs window has a 1.75% variance excess.

Physical pseudo-data therefore need a validated positive-intensity construction that preserves the signed prediction. Discarding negative events or replacing their weights by absolute values would alter the prediction.
