Second push on five other draws

In 4 of five previously trained width-64 development draws, the second push increased the mean shape distance from that draw’s first push. The median paired change was +0.186 percentage points. All five used the same nominal subtraction and development truth reference and took part in the original hyperparameter scan. This comparison describes draw dependence. Repeating iterations on member 47 alone cannot establish the origin of its discrepancy or uncertainty coverage at later iterations.
